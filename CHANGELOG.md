# Release notes

## Unreleased

- **Feedback or report a problem** (new): a link at the bottom of the popup opens a GitHub form with the extension version, Chrome version, and language filled in. Nothing is sent unless you submit it.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-23

First public version.

- **Hide clutter on every tab**: the promoted banner, footer links, Today's puzzles, Premium promos, and the floating Messaging bar.
- **Consistent width**: the same content width on Network, Jobs, Messaging, and Notifications, and a wider Home feed.
- **Block promoted** (off by default): hides promoted posts in the Home feed and shows a count in the popup.
- **Connection filter**: show or hide Home feed posts from 1st, 2nd, and 3rd+ connections, and from everyone else.
- **Hide people at my company**: hides Network suggestions whose headline names a company you enter. The names stay in your browser.
